## "Why do we do knowledge work once?"

Original candidate article frame:

> Why do we do knowledge work once?

Good insight:

- knowledge work historically converged to one run because human effort was expensive;
- AI makes repeat, branch, replay, and comparison cheaper;
- an extra run can produce both artifact value and learning value.

Why it moved out of the title:

- "Why Is Telemetry" is sharper and broader;
- "knowledge work once" becomes a consequence of cognitive observability, not the spine.

## Managed pluralism

Strong Labs concept:

> The future is not less discipline. It is disciplined pluralism.

Meaning:

- multiple workflows where they produce learning or better case-specific outcomes;
- common data, identity, permissions, observability, and governance;
- no reflexive cutover from old process to new process when parallel variants can generate evidence;
- sometimes no single winner, only portfolio allocation by case type.

Possible future title:

> The End of the One Best Way

Caution: overstates if read as anti-standardization. The actual claim is that standardization becomes a design choice, not a default necessity.

## Objective expansion

The objective is not always fixed. AI can reveal adjacent feasible outcomes.

Example:

- start with a general report;
- discover the same research can support a Spanish banking version, Italian healthcare version, Midwest manufacturing version, customer-specific version, interactive tool, API, course, or continuous intelligence feed.

Working distinction:

- objective replacement can cause thrashing;
- objective expansion can be legitimate when capability or cost changes;
- objective changes need evidence thresholds and decision rights.

- Labs framework;

- strategic intelligence tracker model.

## Computation and economics
