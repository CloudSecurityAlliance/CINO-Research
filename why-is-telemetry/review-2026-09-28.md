# External review, 2026-09-28: findings and what we did

Before publication, the column and this research package were reviewed adversarially by a second
AI (GPT-6, via Codex). It was given the column, the claim and decision ledgers, the source sweep,
and this repository. It was asked to check correctness, consistency, audience and style, and not
to rewrite. The full report stays in CSA's internal workspace, because it cites private working
history. Its findings, and what we did about each, are public here.

## Findings on the column

| Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|
| "Never grade the reasoning" went beyond the cited evidence. The study shows that *rewarding* a model for its reasoning drives it to hide misbehavior; it does not show that *reviewing* reasoning is harmful. The column's own example depends on reviewing a recorded reason | **Accepted.** Now "never reward the reasoning for sounding good. Reviewing it is the point; rewarding it is the trap." Also corrected in `TEMPLATE.md` |
| Scoring one expectation against one outcome cannot tell you whether a judgment was good; luck matters | **Accepted.** Now "scoring expectations, over time…; any single result can be luck" |
| The white-text example called the recorded reason *wrong*. It was *incomplete*: the converter really can't reliably detect concealed white text, and the record left out the deliberate trade-off | **Accepted** |
| The novelty claim needed a boundary. Research has proposed agents that compare predictions with outcomes (e.g. *Causal Reflection with Language Models*, Aryan & Liu, 2025, a framework paper) | **Accepted.** The column now acknowledges the research, and scopes "could not find" to the standards and tools organizations use |
| The close never said to act on what you learn | **Accepted.** Now "…and change what I do next because of it" |
| Smaller points: "the why lived in the code" (it's the logic); the Army's review compares against the *plan*, not a forecast; the stylesheet result held only on the email path tested; "shared" security controls; absolutes about history and David; "self-reported estimates" | **All accepted** |

## Findings on this package

| Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|
| An export included internal support-ticket figures from our corpus study, against this repo's own rule | **Accepted.** Removed 2026-09-29 and disclosed in the topic README's Corrections table. History not rewritten |

## What we did not change

- **The column's structure, title, protected lines, and length decision.** The review recommended
  keeping them.
- **The three-question gate for writing a record.** The review agreed it fits as the author's own
  rule; a broader sampling policy belongs in future research.
